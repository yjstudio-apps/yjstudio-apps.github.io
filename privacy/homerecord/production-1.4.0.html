1. 적용 범위와 기기 내 기록

YJ Studio는 이사메모(MovingMemo, 이전 이름 집기록, Android 패키지 com.homerecord.app)에서 다음과 같이 개인정보를 처리합니다. 회원가입과 로그인이 없으며, 개발자는 이용자의 집 기록을 보관하는 서버를 운영하지 않습니다.

집 주소·사진·메모·계약 정보·집주인 이름·이사 일정·계량기·하자 이력·자금 계획·비용과 대출의 이름·원금·이율·기간·상환 방식·첫 납부일은 기기에 저장됩니다. 선택한 국가·언어·통화도 기기에 저장됩니다. 기록 내용이나 금융 입력값을 광고 SDK·은행·공식 안내 사이트·번역 서비스에 보내지 않습니다. 계산은 기기 안에서 이루어지며 계좌 연결, 신용 조회, 대출 신청·중개·승인을 제공하지 않습니다.

2. 이용자가 선택하는 내보내기와 외부 연결

  • 사진·PDF·ZIP 백업의 공유와 복원은 이용자가 직접 선택합니다. 백업에는 집 기록, 자금·비용과 대출 정보가 포함됩니다. 공유·저장 대상으로 고른 서비스에는 그 파일이 전달됩니다. 백업은 자동 클라우드 동기화가 아니며 ZIP 파일은 암호화되지 않으므로 안전하게 보관해 주세요.
  • Android 클라우드 자동 백업에서는 앱 기록을 제외합니다. 기기 간 직접 전송은 운영체제 방식에 따라 가능할 수 있습니다. 앱 삭제·기기 변경 전에는 직접 백업해 주세요.
  • 지도 보기·주변 검색·길찾기를 선택하면 입력한 주소나 동네 이름이 선택한 카카오맵·네이버 지도·Google 지도·AMap에 전달됩니다. 화면을 열거나 주소를 저장하는 것만으로는 전송하지 않습니다. GPS 위치 권한을 요청하거나 위치를 추적하지 않으며 사진·메모·금액은 지도 링크에 포함하지 않습니다.
  • 공식 지원 정보 링크를 열면 외부 브라우저와 해당 사이트의 방침이 적용됩니다. 자금 계획과 입력한 금액은 링크에 포함하지 않습니다. 외부 사이트에 직접 입력하는 정보는 그 서비스의 방침을 확인해 주세요.

1. Scope and records on your device

YJ Studio provides MovingMemo (이사메모, formerly 집기록; Android package com.homerecord.app). There is no account or sign-in. We do not operate a server that receives your home records.

Your home address, photos, notes, contract details, landlord name, moving dates, meter readings, issue history, funds, costs and loan names, principal, rate, term, repayment method and payment dates stay in the app's storage on your device. Country, language and currency preferences are also stored locally. Record contents and entered financial amounts are not sent to advertisers, banks, resource websites or translation services. Calculations run locally. The app does not connect bank accounts, check credit, accept or broker loan applications, or approve credit.

2. Sharing and external services you choose

  • You choose when to share photos, PDFs or ZIP backups, and where to send or restore them. Backups include your records, funds, costs and loans. The receiving service handles the file under its own policy. There is no automatic cloud sync. ZIP backups are not encrypted; store them securely.
  • App records are excluded from Android cloud backup. Direct device-to-device transfer may depend on the operating system. Make your own backup before uninstalling or changing devices.
  • When you choose map search, nearby search or directions, your entered address or area is passed to the selected KakaoMap, Naver Map, Google Maps or AMap service. Merely saving an address or opening the map screen does not send it. The app does not request GPS location or track you. Photos, notes and financial amounts are not included in map links.
  • Official housing and funding links open in an external browser. We do not attach your financial plan or amounts to these links. Information you enter directly on an external site is governed by that site's policy.

3. Advertising and text-recognition SDKs

Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform may process advertising and app-set identifiers or other device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, app launches and ad interactions, and app/SDK diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and privacy choices. The app requests non-personalized ads; this does not mean the SDK collects no information.

Google ML Kit may process device and installation identifiers, device/OS details, app package and version, performance and API usage metrics for diagnostics and usage analytics. Meter text recognition runs on-device: the meter photos themselves are not sent.

Google handles its data under the Google Privacy Policy and its Mobile Ads and ML Kit disclosures. SDK transmissions are encrypted using TLS/HTTPS. Google's policies govern retention and deletion of data it handles.

The app excludes the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland from ads. It conservatively checks selected/device regions and UMP status, blocking ads when privacy choices are required or status checks fail. If the SDK requires a privacy-options entry point, you can open More → Privacy → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset/delete your advertising ID in Android's Google advertising settings. Record features remain available without ads.

Ad-removal purchases are deferred. This version does not query in-app products or start purchase/restoration flows, and does not ask you to enter payment details.

4. Permissions

Camera access is optional for taking photos. Notifications are optional for moving-task reminders. Declining them limits the related feature only. Internet access supports ads, privacy checks and the external links you choose. No location, contacts or microphone permission is requested.

5. Retention, deletion and choices

Local records remain until you delete them. Deleting a home removes its related records, including loans. Use More → Privacy → Delete all data, or uninstall the app, to remove local records. We have no server copy. Exported files and data handled by external services must be managed separately. You can view, edit, back up and delete your records in the app.

6. Children, changes and contact

The app is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Policy changes will be posted here and, when needed, in the store listing.
