Evidence Note

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 23, 2026 · YJ Studio · 한국어

YJ Studio ("the developer") handles the personal information of users of the Evidence Note app ("the app") as described below, in line with applicable laws including Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.

1. Basic principles

  • The app has no sign-up or sign-in.
  • Everything you keep in the app — case details (name, place, description), notes, contact logs (calls, messages, and visits you write down yourself, including the other party's name and contact details), photos, videos, recordings, and documents — is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer's servers. The developer cannot see these records.
  • The app is set up so that this data is not copied to your device's cloud backup (Google backup) either.
  • A SHA-256 value is stored with each file. It is a value for checking whether the file's content has changed since it was stored, and it stays on your device.

2. Information that leaves your device (collected by third parties)

Your records never leave your device. However, the advertising service included in the app automatically collects the information below.

Apart from the services above, the developer does not provide your information to third parties and does not outsource the processing of personal information.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Records on your device are kept until you delete them.
  • Deleted records move to Trash. Deleting them permanently from Trash also removes the original files and related files such as thumbnails.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all records on your device, and the developer keeps no copies. Backup files you made yourself must be deleted by you.

5. Your rights

  • You can view, edit, and delete your records in the app at any time.
  • Because the developer does not keep your records, there is no data held by the developer to access or delete. If you have questions, contact us below.

6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

7. Security measures

  • Records are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • Information sent to the advertising service is encrypted in transit (TLS).
  • You can turn on app lock (fingerprint, face, or screen lock) and hide the app in recent apps. App lock stops others from opening the app; the stored files themselves are protected by your device's built-in encryption.
  • Backup files and ZIP files with originals are not encrypted, so take care when sharing or storing them.
